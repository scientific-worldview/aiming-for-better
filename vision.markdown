### A Dream

The ultimate _vision_{: .neon} of this website is _world peace_{: .bold} and an end to all **war**, **violence**, and **unnecessary suffering**.

![Dream]({{ "/assets/images/dream.webp" | relative_url }})

We _dream_{: .neon} that a *genuinely better* world is within our reach, but it cannot be built upon the **fragile foundations** of [dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dogma), tribal loyalties, or _unquestioned_{: .warn} tradition. As outlined in our [mission-statement]({{ "/mission-statement/" | relative_url }}), the only _reliable_{: .cool} path to realising this goal is through the universal adoption of a *scientific worldview*. By applying a rigorous, evolutionary, and evidence-based approach to how we organize society, politics, and law, we can systematically dismantle the engines of conflict and build a framework for lasting global harmony.

_We dream of a future where humanity finally outgrows the self-destructive instincts of its past._{: .subtle} 

For too long, the world has operated *upside down* and *inside out*. We are a species that *obsesses* over the microscopic, micromanaged risks of daily life while remaining **willfully blind** to the massive, existential threats of *our own making*. We are guided by a highly risk-averse [secular priesthood]({{ "/guide/obstacles/secular-priesthood/" | relative_url }})—bureaucrats, media pundits, and managers in business suits—who _dictate_{: .warn} the values of a **"healthy society"** to keep us _distracted_{: .bold} by minor infractions while systemic dangers loom.

We _dream_{: .neon} of a [society]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/" | relative_url }}) that looks back at the tragedy of the 20th century—the slaughter of over 100 million human beings—_and truly learns its lesson_{: .mark}. We must *recognise* that the greatest threat to our [survival]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#survival) is not an external force, but our persistent clinging to the nation-state, our deeply ingrained [in-group versus out-group]({{ "/case-studies/us-vs-them/" | relative_url }}) tribalism, and the [locked-in]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }}) historical [narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) we _refuse to question_{: .mark}. 

To _survive_{: .cool} and _thrive_{: .cool}, we must stop gathering to cheer the very **war machines** that could *engineer our end*. We must **refuse** to let hard-won freedoms slip away through jingoistic scapegoating and manufactured [fear-mongering]({{ "/guide/obstacles/fear-mongering/" | relative_url }}) designed to serve the vested interests of a few.

### A New Framework for Society

For such a _dream_{: .neon} to come true, this future requires a *fundamental shift* in how we process information and govern ourselves. We must _dream_{: .neon} of a world where a rigorous, scientific worldview is consistently applied to society, politics, and law. 

By dreaming of such a rational, evidence-based process for thinking to [human behaviour]({{ "/guide/human-history/psychology/" | relative_url }}), we can strip away the comforting illusions of jingoism and archaic traditions. We can dismantle the narratives that divide us into warring tribes and instead build a society grounded in _universal freedom_{: .cool}—freedom for everyone, not just those within a specific in-group.

The _dream_{: .neon} of this site is to help _pioneer_{: .bold} that shift: to encourage a collective awakening where we finally address [the big risks]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2026-06-03-big-risks %}), challenge the entrenched elites, and _build a sane, rational world_{: .mark}.
