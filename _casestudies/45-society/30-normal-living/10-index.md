### Overview

Welcome to the series. In this _exploration_{: .cool} of "Normal" Living, we turn our attention to the *everyday structures of modern life* that are so familiar they have become _invisible_{: .bold}. We are told from birth that growing up in a private household, being raised by one or two parents, and adhering to strict societal conventions are the **"normal"** _default settings_{: .terminal} for human beings.

_But are they?_{: .subtle}

This series *challenges* the _assumption_{: .warn} that our current way of living is biologically or historically [natural]({{ "/guide/human-history/natural-vs-normal/" | relative_url }}). By _examining_{: .cool} our daily environments through the lens of evolutionary history, we will reveal that much of what modern society accepts as standard is, in fact, _a radical and very recent social experiment_{: .mark}.

### The Evolutionary Mismatch: When Normal is Unnatural

For **millions of years**, earlier humans and modern humans ([Homo sapiens]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#modern-humans-homo-sapiens)) evolved to live in cooperative, communal bands. We thrived on distributed childcare, shared resources, and high levels of physical and social autonomy. Our biology and psychology are _fundamentally adapted_{: .bold} (see [Adaptation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#adaptation)) to this rich, interconnected _village ecosystem_{: .cool}.

However, the [Agricultural Transition]({{ "/guide/human-history/agricultural-transition/" | relative_url }}) and the subsequent [Industrial Revolution]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#industrialisation) _completely overhauled our environment_{: .mark}. We were _segmented_{: .warn} into **isolated** [nuclear families]({{ "/case-studies/society/normal-living/family/" | relative_url }}), disconnected from extended kinship networks, and placed into highly structured, institutionalised routines. This created a profound [evolutionary mismatch]({{ "/case-studies/mismatches/" | relative_url }}). Our _environments_{: .bold} *changed drastically*, but our [innate]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#innate) _biological requirements_{: .bold} *did not*.

The _consequences_{: .warn} of this mismatch are **everywhere**, yet they are _routinely ignored_{: .bold} because they are baked into the societal definition of normal. We [pathologise]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#pathologize) natural human behaviours while _remaining entirely blind to the systemic neglect inherent in isolating humans from their evolutionary need for_{: .mark} [community]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#community).

### The Goal: Redefining Harm and Human Flourishing

The overarching goal of this '"Normal" Living' series is to:

- **Expose** the _artificial nature_{: .bold} of modern living arrangements, specifically the isolated nuclear family and our current models of child-rearing.

- **Deconstruct** _socially constructed_{: .bold} definitions of harm, neglect, and abuse, replacing them with objective, scientifically grounded metrics *based on human biology*.

- **Highlight** the _hidden systemic mistreatment_{: .bold} that occurs when we deprive individuals, especially children, of a wider community and natural [autonomy]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#autonomy).

- **Provide** a rational, _evidence-based framework_{: .cool} for _designing environments that actually align with our evolved_{: .mark} [human nature]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#human-nature).

Ultimately, we aim to strip away the cultural [normalisation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#normalisation) of these _high-risk_{: .warn} _social experiments_{: .bold}. By _understanding_{: .cool} what *our biology actually requires*, we can stop measuring [well-being]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#wellbeing) by **arbitrary** social [consensus]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#consensus) and start building systems designed for *genuine* [human flourishing]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#human-flourishing).
