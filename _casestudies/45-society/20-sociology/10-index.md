### Overview

Welcome to the series. In this **"The Science of Human Society"** exploration, we delve into the *core concepts* and enduring questions of how humans organise themselves into societies. Traditionally, [sociology]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/" | relative_url }}) has sought to describe and explain social patterns, institutions, and behaviours. However, it has often done so with a **"notable absence"**: the insights offered by [scientific research]({{ "/guide/first-principles/scientific-method/" | relative_url }}), (neuroscience/genetics/[epigenetics]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#epigenetics)), [evolutionary]({{ "/guide/human-history/" | relative_url }}) psychology, and sometimes the *scientific method itself*.

![Sociology]({{ "/assets/images/casestudies/society/sociology.webp" | relative_url }})

This series challenges the *traditional boundaries* of sociological thought. We will systematically re-examine the *fundamental building blocks* of human society—from the invisible rules that guide our interactions to the [hierarchies]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#hierarchies) we form, the groups we belong to, and the very ways we learn to be human—all through the rigorous, [evidence-based]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}) lens of a [scientific worldview]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#scientific-worldview). Our journey will reveal that social phenomena are not merely *cultural constructs* or arbitrary arrangements, but *emergent properties* shaped by our _evolved_{: .bold} [human nature]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#human-nature) interacting with specific environmental and [intersubjective]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }}) contexts.

### The Scientific Imperative: Grounding Society in Evolutionary Reality

Human societies are *complex adaptive systems*. To truly understand them, we must move beyond purely *descriptive accounts* or ideologically driven interpretations. This series embraces a _scientific imperative_{: .bold}: to ground our understanding of sociology in [testable]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }}) hypotheses, [empirical]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#empirical) evidence, and the *profound insights* of [evolution]({{ "/guide/human-history/evolution/" | relative_url }}) and [natural selection]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#natural-selection).

We will explicitly integrate principles from evolutionary psychology, neuroscience, and behavioural economics to illuminate the **"why"** behind social structures, not just the **"what"**. This involves recognizing our **"pre-patterned slate"**—the [innate]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#innate) predispositions, learning [biases]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/cognitive-biases/" | relative_url }}), and *social instincts* that make us uniquely susceptible to [socialisation]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/socialisation/" | relative_url }}) and group formation, yet also set the constraints and guide the *pathways of our social lives*. By doing so, we aim to dissect the powerful **"**[Mismatches]({{ "/case-studies/mismatches/" | relative_url }})**"** between our ancient, [evolved psychology]({{ "/guide/human-history/psychology/" | relative_url }}) and the demands of modern, large-scale societies, and to expose the persistent **"**[Lock-Ins]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }})**"** that prevent [rational]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#rational) adaptation and perpetuate [dysfunction]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dysfunction). This scientific approach will allow us to transform sociology from a primarily descriptive discipline into one capable of objective analysis, prediction, and ultimately, the *intentional design* of more adaptive and [flourishing]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#human-flourishing) human societies.

### The Goal: Revealing Society's DNA, Not Just Debating Its Surface

The overarching goal of this **'Rebuilding Sociology on Scientific Foundations'** series is to:

- Establish a _scientific vocabulary_{: .bold} and framework for understanding human social [behaviour]({{ "/guide/human-history/psychology/" | relative_url }}) and organisation, explicitly integrating evolutionary and evidence-based perspectives.

- Deconstruct *traditional sociological concepts* (norms, [status]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/status/" | relative_url }}), identity, socialisation, major theories) and rigorously test them against objective evidence and evolutionary insights.

- Expose the **"Mismatches"** and **"Lock-Ins"** that arise when *evolved human nature* clashes with complex modern social structures.

- Empower readers with a critical, *scientifically-informed lens* to analyse social phenomena, question [Prevailing Narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}), and identify pathways for _societal improvement_{: .cool}.

Ultimately, we aim to move beyond partial, often ideologically driven, explanations to a comprehensive, testable, and actionable understanding of human [society]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/" | relative_url }}) that can guide us in _building a future aligned with objective reality_{: .mark} and universal human [well-being]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#wellbeing).
