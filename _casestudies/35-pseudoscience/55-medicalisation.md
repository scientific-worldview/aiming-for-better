### Historical Perspective: The Graveyard of Medical Consensus

Before we unquestioningly accept the authority of today's doctors and psychiatrists, we must remember that the "respectable" medical establishment has a **devastating history** of confusing cultural [dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dogma) with scientific fact. Historical medical consensus is frequently a graveyard of _horrific_{: .warn} [pseudoscience]({{ "/case-studies/pseudoscience/" | relative_url }}):

#### Ignaz Semmelweis and Handwashing

In the 1840s, Semmelweis proved that doctors washing their hands *drastically reduced* maternal mortality. The medical establishment mocked and ostracized him, refusing to believe **"gentlemen's hands"** could transmit disease.

#### Bloodletting and Trepanation

For centuries, the highest medical authorities confidently drilled holes in skulls or drained patients' blood to balance _imaginary_{: .warn} "humours".

#### X-Rays During Pregnancy

In the mid-20th century, doctors routinely used high-dose X-rays on pregnant women, *utterly blind* to the _catastrophic_{: .warn} cellular damage they were causing.

#### Stomach Ulcers (H. pylori)

For decades, the **medical consensus** stated that ulcers were "caused" by stress and spicy food. It took Dr. Barry Marshall deliberately drinking a beaker of *Helicobacter pylori* in 1984 to prove _the establishment was ignoring basic bacterial biology_{: .mark}.

#### Dietary Cholesterol and Trans Fats

The mid-century medical panic over natural saturated fats led **the establishment** to actively promote highly processed, artificial trans fats as a "heart-healthy" alternative—_a mistake that objectively killed millions before being reversed_{: .mark}.

#### The Medicalisation of Sexuality

Until 1973, the **psychiatric establishment** officially *classified* homosexuality as a _sociopathic_{: .bold} **"mental illness"**, "curing" it with chemical castration and electroshock therapy.

The lesson *is clear*: just because a man in a white coat holds a "prestigious" degree does not mean his **"diagnosis"** is based on empirical reality.

_Frequently, he is merely enforcing the cultural norms of his era._{: .subtle}

### Step 2: Applying First Principles

#### Testability & The DSM

_An evidence-first approach exposes the glaring lack of biology in modern behavioral medicine:_{: .subtle}

Achieving intellectual [emancipation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#emancipation) means we must *fiercely celebrate* the brilliant scientists and doctors who cure actual biological diseases, while remaining **eternally vigilant** against _the institutions that attempt to pathologise the human condition_{: .mark}.
