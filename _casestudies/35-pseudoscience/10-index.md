### An Introduction to the Series

What happens when a powerful [narrative]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) needs to defend itself in a world that increasingly respects the authority of science? It doesn't abandon its story.

_It puts on a lab coat_{: .subtle}

This is the birth of _pseudoscience_{: .warn}: a collection of claims, [beliefs]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#beliefs), or practices *presented as scientific*, but which are *incompatible* with the [scientific method]({{ "/guide/first-principles/scientific-method/" | relative_url }}). It is the most insidious and dangerous form of the [Lock-In]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }}) Effect. It _hijacks the credibility of science to legitimise a pre-determined narrative_{: .mark}, creating a fortress of **"expert opinion"** that is incredibly difficult for the public—and even other scientists—to tear down.

![Pseudoscience]({{ "/assets/images/casestudies/ps/ps.webp" | relative_url }})

This series of case studies will explore this *recurring and destructive pattern*. We will see how powerful _vested interests_{: .bold}—from colonial empires to modern therapeutic industries—have repeatedly used *the language and authority of science* not to discover truth, but to **enforce** a _narrative_{: .warn} that *serves* their own [power]({{ "/case-studies/politics/governance/power/" | relative_url }}), profit, or ideology_{: .bold}.

### The Blueprint of the Pseudoscience Lock-In

Unlike a Brute Force _Lock-In_{: .neon} that relies on open violence, or a Metaphysical Lock-In that relies on [untestable faith]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }}), the **Pseudoscience Lock-In** operates through a more *subtle* and *modern* form of _deception_{: .warn}. The blueprint is remarkably consistent:

- _Start with a Narrative:_{: .bullet} A powerful group has *a pre-existing goal or belief*. For example, "Our race is superior and destined to rule", or "This specific [psychological therapy]({{ "/guide/human-history/therapy-culture/" | relative_url }}) is the cure for crime".

- _Generate "Evidence":_{: .bullet} The group then funds or promotes research that is designed _not to test_{: .bold} this narrative, *but* _to confirm it_{: .bold}. This often involves *flawed methodologies*, *cherry-picked data*, and a *refusal to consider alternative explanations*.

- _Co-opt the Language of Science:_{: .bullet} The findings are published in official-sounding journals or presented by "experts" with impressive credentials. They are filled with *technical jargon*, [statistics]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#statistics), and *complex charts*, all designed to create an overwhelming _impression of scientific legitimacy_{: .bold}.

- _Create a Professional Class:_{: .bullet} An [institution]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/institutional-control/" | relative_url }}) or professional class is created whose *identity and livelihood* are _based_{: .bold} on this "scientific" narrative. This creates *a powerful vested interest*.

- _Pathologise Dissent:_{: .bullet} Anyone who *questions the narrative* or points out the *flaws in the [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}) is not engaged in a scientific debate. They are branded as "unscientific", "ideologically motivated", "anti-progress", or a _"denier"_{: .bold}.
 
_The scientific process of skepticism is reframed as a moral or intellectual failing._{: .subtle}

This creates a powerful, *self-sealing system*. The "science" justifies the institution, and the institution's experts become the **sole arbiters** of what is considered valid "science", effectively *locking out any genuine, evidence-based critique*.

### What Follows: A Series of Case Studies

In the case studies that follow, we will examine this **Pseudoscience Lock-In** across different historical and modern contexts. We will explore:

- _Colonial Administrations:_{: .bullet} How the _pseudoscience_{: .neon} of "scientific racism" was used to create racial [hierarchies]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#hierarchies) that *justified colonial rule and [exploitation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#exploitation).

- _The Eugenics Movement:_{: .bullet} How *flawed interpretations of genetics* were used to **"prove"** the inferiority of certain groups, leading to forced sterilisation and racist immigration [laws]({{ "/case-studies/law/theory/" | relative_url }}).

- _The "Maternal Deprivation" Hypothesis:_{: .bullet} How *a selective interpretation of psychological theory* was used to create a "scientific" _justification_{: .warn} for pushing women out of the workforce and back into [traditional]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#tradition) [domestic]({{ "/case-studies/society/normal-living/" | relative_url }}) roles.

- _The "Recovered Memory" Hysteria:_{: .bullet} How *a dangerous and unscientific therapeutic model created a* [moral panic]({{ "/case-studies/politics/dysfunction/moral-panics/" | relative_url }}) that destroyed _innocent lives_{: .cool}, all under the _banner_{: .warn} of psychological "healing".

- _The Offender Treatment Industry:_{: .bullet} How the modern [justice]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#justice-and-injustice) system has become _locked_{: .neon} into a *multi-million pound* _industry_{: .warn} of **"evidence-based"** psychological programmes, many of which, when subjected to rigorous testing, _are found to be ineffective or even counter-productive_{: .mark}.

The lesson from this series is clear. A true _scientific worldview_{: .neon} is not about blindly trusting anyone who calls themselves a scientist or any study that is filled with data.

_It is a commitment to a process of relentless skepticism._{: .mark}

> It is the understanding that the scientific method is not a body of [facts]({{ "/guide/first-principles/correct-answers/" | relative_url }}), but *a method for questioning*—and that the most important time to use that method is *when a powerful group tells you that the science is "settled"*.
