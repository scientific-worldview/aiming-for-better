_The World is Getting Better, But..._{: .retro}

### Why This Project Exists

The aim of this project is simple but _ambitious_{: .bold}: _to promote a scientific, evolutionary, and evidence-based approach to thinking_{: .mark}—not just in laboratories, but across [society]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/" | relative_url }}), [politics]({{ "/case-studies/politics/democracy/" | relative_url }}), [morality]({{ "/case-studies/morality/" | relative_url }}), and [law]({{ "/case-studies/law/theory/" | relative_url }}).

![Dream]({{ "/assets/images/mission-dream.webp" | relative_url }})

We believe that the [scientific method]({{ "/articles/science/ebsm/" | relative_url }}), with its [clean-slate]({{ "/guide/clean-slate/" | relative_url }}) _mindset_{: .bold}, demand for [testability]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }}), insistence on **reliable** [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}), and *openness to correction*, is humanity’s _most powerful tool for understanding the world and improving it._{: .mark} 

Crucially, "science" here does not just mean physics and chemistry. It means rigorously applying *evolutionary anthropology*, *biology*, and *objective sociology* to the _critical decisions_{: .bold} that shape our everyday lives. Too often, [society]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/" | relative_url }}) ignores the profound clash between our ancient _biological hardware_{: .terminal} and our highly artificial modern _social software_{: .terminal}. 

_This project seeks to change that._{: .subtle}

---

### The Deeper Mission

We are **not** here to push a fixed _ideology_{: .warn}, *political tribe*, or *moral code*. 

We are here to _champion a better way of thinking._{: .mark}

- A way of thinking that rigorously separates **objective reality** from [intersubjective fictions]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }})—the made-up laws, norms, and titles that society pretends are laws of nature.
- A way of thinking that *questions* [inherited beliefs]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#beliefs), however _popular_{: .bold} or [sacred]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#sacred).
- A way of thinking that **demands** *clarity*, *coherence*, and [empirical]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#empirical) accountability.
- A way of thinking that starts from *"I don’t know"* and moves forward with _care_{: .bold} and [humility]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/intellectual-humility/" | relative_url }}).

If more people, institutions, and governments applied this kind of thinking consistently, we believe the world would see:

- **Fewer harmful laws** based on outdated [dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dogma).
- **Fewer rights denied** because of [stories]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) and _myths_{: .bold}.
- **Fewer policies shaped by ideology**, and more shaped by [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}).
- **Fewer false debates**, and more _progress_{: .bold} on *real problems*.
- **More lives improved**, and less unnecessary _suffering_{: .warn}.

Even if you disagree with aspects or conclusions of this website (and the scientific method demands we welcome challenge and dissent), if it encourages you to question, to think again, to hypothesise, and not just "accept", then it has served its purpose.

### The Blight of the Secular Priesthood

To understand why this way of thinking is so urgently needed, we must identify what opposes it. For nearly a minellia, we were controlled by the [religious priesthood]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/soul-control/" | relative_url }}). Now, for at least the last century, we have allowed ourselves to be hoodwinked by a self-appointed [secular priesthood]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#secular-priesthood) of bureaucrats, moral arbiters, institutional managers, and legal authorities that now dictate how human beings must live. 

Operating entirely on unevidenced [narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}), this class has routinely overridden our biological realities to protect their own power and [status quo]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#status-quo). What gives them the right to dictate to the rest of us when we have science and evidence on our side? *Nothing but culturally enforced [obedience]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#obedience).*

Historically and currently, this secular priesthood is responsible for devastating societal blights. Because they [frame]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#framing) artificial rules as absolute morality, they have justified horrific systemic abuses—from the cruel institutionalisation of unmarried mothers and the forced migration of vulnerable children to Australia, to the pseudoscientific [Offender Treatment Industry]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#offender-treatment-industry) of today. They pathologise natural human friction, demand blind compliance, and severely punish [diversity]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#diversity).

_This project explicitly challenges their unearned authority._{: .mark}

### Why History Matters

[Human history]({{ "/guide/human-history/" | relative_url }}) reveals a clear pattern. For almost **ALL** of our existence, *Homo sapiens* and earlier humans lived horizontally within [egalitarian]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#egalitarian) foraging bands. But following the **[Agricultural Transition]({{ "/guide/human-history/agricultural-transition/" | relative_url }})**, the rise of massive, stratified civilization brought not just _order_{: .bold}, but systemic [oppression]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#oppression) justified by [invented narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }})—[religious dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#religion), divine kingship, racial [hierarchies]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#hierarchies), restrictive [gender roles]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#gender-role), and criminalized [sexuality]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#sexuality).

If the _ethos of science_{: .neon} had been applied earlier—*truly applied*—then tragedies like [slavery]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#slavery), the _oppression_{: .warn} of women, and the _persecution_{: .warn} of minorities might have ended **centuries sooner**, or perhaps *never occurred at all*.

We cannot change the past. **But we can change how we think today.** 

_And that is the only real hope for changing the future._{: .subtle}

### Why Case Studies Matter

The site’s [Case Studies]({{ "/case-studies/" | relative_url }}) apply this [rational approach]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#rational) to modern and historical _controversies_{: .warn}. We don’t argue from _ideology_{: .warn}—we begin with a [clean slate]({{ "/guide/clean-slate/" | relative_url }}) and apply [first principles]({{ "/guide/first-principles/" | relative_url }}).

We ask: What is [testable]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }})? What is [objective reality]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }}) versus intersubjective fiction? What is the actual [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }})? _And what happens when we discard [myth]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#myth) and [agenda]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#agendas) and simply try to get it right?_{: .mark}

We’re not afraid to examine [abortion]({{ "/case-studies/law/pragmatic/abortion/" | relative_url }}), [drugs]({{ "/case-studies/law/pragmatic/drug-laws/" | relative_url }}), [hate speech]({{ "/case-studies/law/pragmatic/hate-speech/" | relative_url }}), [democracy]({{ "/case-studies/politics/democracy/" | relative_url }}), **crime**, or any other _divisive_{: .warn} issue—because what matters most is *not the opinion*, but **how that opinion is formed**.

### Our Core Belief

In short, this project believes:

_The scientific approach is the best—and perhaps only—reliable yardstick for improving the human condition for all._{: .subtle}

If more people *embraced this approach*, many of the _errors_{: .warn} and _injustices_{: .warn} of our past could be **avoided in the future**. True human flourishing can only occur when we stop punishing people for their [biology]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#biology), and instead redesign our social software to accommodate our evolved needs. By doing this, lives could be _improved_{: .bold} earlier, suffering could be _reduced_{: .bold} sooner, and *better outcomes could emerge*—**not** by _force_{: .warn} or _ideology_{: .warn}, but by [thinking clearly]({{ "/guide/" | relative_url }}), *acting rationally*, and _being honest about what we know and what we don’t._{: .mark}

### The Question of Realism

It is **inevitable** that some readers will view the systemic shifts implicit in our arguments as hopelessly _romantic_{: .cool} or entirely _unworkable_{: .bold}. In a complex world governed by _realpolitik_{: .bold} and historical [tribalism]({{ "/case-studies/politics/political-dynamics/tribalism/" | relative_url }}), discarding [traditional]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#tradition) structures in favour of a purely rational, [first-principles]({{ "/guide/first-principles/" | relative_url }}) society might even seem _dangerous_{: .warn}—a naive _idealism_{: .cool} that could leave a nation vulnerable to those who still play by the old, ruthless rules. _It is fair to ask: are we simply proposing an impossible utopia?_{: .mark}

To borrow a _famous sentiment_{: .bold}, you might say we are _dreamers_{: .cool}, but we know **we are not the only ones**. The primary objective of this website is not to draft an executable, overnight manifesto for global governments or to demand the immediate, reckless dismantling of existing [institutions]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/institutional-control/" | relative_url }}). The real point is to **make people think**, and to _encourage_{: .bold} them to *"challenge and dissent"* (see below). By _illuminating_{: .cool} the **arbitrary nature** of our [societal norms]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/norms/" | relative_url }}) and exploring what a truly [evidence-based]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}) civilization *might look like*, we seek to **raise profound questions** and awaken a **broader public awareness**.

We *harbour no illusions* about the friction of human [progress]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/progress/" | relative_url }}). The kind of foundational changes explored in these pages may not materialize within our lifetimes. Yet, _progress_{: .bold} relies entirely on the **courage to imagine it**. This project is anchored in the *possibility of change* and the _aspiration_{: .bold} that by continually challenging our collective _constraints_{: .warn}, we can **plant the seeds for tomorrow**. However distant that horizon may be, the _goal_{: .bold} remains a **demonstrably better world** where, eventually, human beings can live richer, more [flourishing]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#human-flourishing) lives.

### Challenge and Dissent

This project is *not an exposition of facts*; it is an _active_{: .bold} and _deliberate_{: .bold} _challenge_{: .warn} to the [status quo]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#status-quo). The _scientific worldview_{: .neon}, at its core, is a **constant process of questioning**, refining, and, when necessary, _overturning established beliefs when faced with superior evidence._{: .mark}

Our aim is to foster this _spirit_{: .bold} of [critical inquiry]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/" | relative_url }}) in every reader, inviting them to dismantle their own [Lock-Ins]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }}) and _confront_{: .bold} [Evolutionary Mismatches]({{ "/case-studies/mismatches/" | relative_url }}) wherever they find them.

While we strive for the utmost _accuracy_{: .bold} and apply our [first principles]({{ "/guide/first-principles/" | relative_url }}) (testability, objective evidence, scientific method) with **rigorous impartiality**, *this project makes no claim to infallibility*. Indeed, to claim otherwise would be _unscientific_{: .bold}. We fully embrace the understanding that our analyses and proposed hypotheses may not always be "right", especially when venturing into complex social and psychological territory where definitive answers remain elusive. And that's *precisely the point*: _to be corrected is not a failure, but a triumph of the scientific method._{: .mark} It demonstrates the _self-correcting_{: .bold} nature of inquiry, pushing us closer to a more **accurate understanding of reality**. 

In order to effectively *challenge and dissent*, a measured tone is sometimes _insufficient_{: .bold}. There will be instances where this project adopts a **polemical stance**, or appears to "exaggerate" a phenomenon, or plays [Devil's Advocate]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#devils-advocate). This is a deliberate rhetorical strategy, akin to using a magnifying glass to [illuminate]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) a subtle, yet profound, _flaw_{: .warn} that might *otherwise remain unseen*. Such an approach is specifically employed when confronting [narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) or **unscientific beliefs** that resist conventional challenge, _aiming to jolt the reader into critical re-evaluation._{: .mark}

This _commitment_{: .bold} to challenge and dissent is not merely an intellectual exercise; **it is a societal imperative**. In a world saturated with [misinformation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#misinformation), *emotional appeals*, and *vested interests* that entrench ["bad ideas"]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }}), the ability to critically question, to _dissent_{: .bold} from the [comfortable consensus]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#consensus), and to demand [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}) is the *bedrock of progress*. This project invites you to join that *critical endeavor*, not just as a passive reader, but as an **active participant** in the *ongoing quest* for a more _enlightened_{: .bold}, _evidence-based understanding of ourselves and the world._{: .mark}

## In Summary

This project exists to promote:

- **Scientific literacy & Evolutionary awareness**
- **Critical thinking & First Principles reasoning**
- **Intellectual humility**
- **Freedom from dogma & the Secular Priesthood**
- **Clearer public reasoning based on Objective reality**
- **A more humane world built on evidence, not ideology**

Along the way we tackle:

- **First Principles:** Building knowledge from the ground up using testability and the [clean slate]({{ "/guide/clean-slate/" | relative_url }}).
- **Objective vs. Intersubjective:** Learning to separate physical, biological reality from socially constructed [legal fictions]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }}).
- **Mismatches:** When our modern societal software _clashes_{: .bold} with our [evolved biological hardware]({{ "/case-studies/mismatches/" | relative_url }}).
- **Narratives:** How [stories]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) and moral framing can lead us _astray_{: .warn}.
- **Lock-Ins:** How *vested interests* and *tradition* keep us locked into [bad ideas]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }}).
- **Pseudoscience:** _False_{: .warn} credibility by _dressing up narratives in a lab-coat_{: .mark}.

_We hope this is the beginning of a better way to think—and a better way to live._{: .subtle}
