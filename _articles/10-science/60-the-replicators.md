If you like, you could call it a scientific origin myth. It is found in chapter two of “The Selfish Gene”, the first book of zoologist and evolutionary biologist Dawkins. I will try to summarise it here:

In this chapter, “The Replicators”, Dawkins introduces the concept of replicators—the first molecules capable of making copies of themselves.

### The Origin of Life and the First Replicators

- Dawkins speculates about prebiotic Earth, where simple organic molecules existed in a primordial soup.
- At some point, a molecule formed by chance that could make copies of itself—this was the first replicator.
> This chance formation was, statistically, an extremely small probability: theoretically, it might never have happened. Yet, given the enormous number of atoms and molecules on Earth and the hundreds upon hundreds of millions of years available, the probability greatly increases.
- These molecules were not alive but began a process that led to the evolution of life.

### Replication and Errors (Mutations) Lead to Evolution

- Replicators made imperfect copies, meaning mutations occurred.
- Some variations were more stable or better at replicating than others.
- Through [natural selection]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#natural-selection), more efficient replicators became dominant.

### Competition and the "Survival of the Stable"

- The primordial soup contained limited resources, so replicators had to compete for molecules to build new copies.
- Only the most stable and efficient replicators survived.
> Natural selection is the process by which traits that enhance [survival]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#survival) and replication/reproduction become more common in a population over generations, while less advantageous traits disappear.
- Natural selection began at the molecular level, long before the existence of cells or organisms.

### The Transition to Complex Life

- Over geological time, replicators became enclosed in protective structures, leading to the first cells.
- These replicators evolved into genes.
- Genes are modern-day replicators, still "competing" for [survival]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#survival) through reproduction.

### Genes are the Units of Evolution

- Dawkins challenges the idea that organisms (or species) are the primary units of evolution.
- Instead, genes are the real "selfish" entities, using organisms as "survival machines" to propagate themselves.

Hence, the purpose of your life, my life, every organism's life, is as a protective vehicle to transport genetic information from one generation to the next.

Survival machines for genes. That’s all any organism is. It matters not to **“mother nature”** if such organisms happen to be sentient or not, are happy or sad, in comfort or agony. Apart from this purpose, we are to all intents and purposes, irrelevant.
