### Drug Policy & Classification (UK Cannabis Reclassification)

- In 2004, the UK downgraded cannabis from Class B to Class C, following scientific recommendations that it posed lower risks than harder drugs.
- In 2009, despite scientific advice, it was reclassified back to Class B under political pressure to appear “tough on drugs.”
- The government’s own Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs (ACMD) opposed the move, and its chairman, Professor David Nutt, was fired after publicly criticizing the decision.

**Priority Over Science:** *Political pressure to appear tough on crime and appease the public, rather than [evidence-based]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}) [harm reduction]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#harm-reduction).*

### COVID-19 Pandemic Response & Delayed Lockdowns

- UK & US governments delayed lockdowns despite early scientific evidence showing that fast action would save lives.
- Some leaders downplayed the virus, prioritizing the economy and public morale over epidemiological warnings.

**Priority Over Science:** *Economic stability and political messaging took precedence over early intervention.*

### Climate Change Policy & Fossil Fuel Interests

- Scientific consensus: Climate change is real, primarily caused by human CO₂ emissions, and urgent action is needed.
- However, governments, particularly in the US, China, and some EU countries, subsidise fossil fuel industries or delay renewable energy transitions due to economic and political pressures.

**Priority Over Science:** Short-term economic growth, job protection in fossil fuel industries, and corporate [lobbying]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#lobbying) override long-term climate science.

### Agricultural Policy & Pesticides (Neonicotinoids & Bees)

- Scientific evidence: Neonicotinoid pesticides harm bee populations, threatening ecosystems and food production.
- The EU initially banned them (2018), but the UK temporarily allowed their use for sugar beet farming in 2021 despite opposition from scientists.

**Priority Over Science:** *Economic interests of farmers and the agricultural industry over environmental protection.*

### Nuclear Energy & Public Fear

- Scientific evidence: Nuclear power is a low-carbon, efficient energy source with a lower death rate than fossil fuels.
- Policy reality: Many countries (e.g., Germany) phase out nuclear energy due to public fears post-Fukushima, leading to increased reliance on coal and gas.

**Priority Over Science:** *Public opinion, [fear]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#fear), and political ideology over energy science.*

### GMOs (Genetically Modified Organisms) & Public Perception

- Scientific consensus: GMOs are safe and can improve food security.
- Policy reality: The EU and some nations impose heavy restrictions due to public distrust and activist pressure.

**Priority Over Science:** *Consumer fears, cultural resistance, and political ideology over scientific evidence.*

### Vaccination & Political Hesitancy (MMR & HPV)

- Scientific evidence: MMR vaccine does not cause autism (as proven after the Wakefield study was debunked).
- Policy hesitancy: Some governments failed to counter [misinformation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#misinformation) quickly, leading to declining vaccination rates.
- HPV vaccine (for cervical cancer) was delayed or restricted in some countries due to moral concerns about [teenage]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/adolescence/" | relative_url }}) [sexual]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#sexuality) activity.

**Priority Over Science:** *Misinformation, public sentiment, and moral concerns over public health data.*
