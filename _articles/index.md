This website is written for curious minds of all backgrounds, not just academics or experts. That means we’ve tried to keep the main pages as clear, straightforward, and jargon-free as possible. We try our best to avoid big words and complex ideas. Not because they’re bad — but because the goal here is understanding and accessibility.

To keep things focused, I’ve tried not to go off on long tangents or overload each page with side notes. But sometimes, a topic deserves a closer look. That’s why this section was included.

Here, you’ll find a growing collection of *optional* asides, summaries, articles or similar "tangents" that dig deeper into certain subjects. These pieces might explore extra detail, offer more [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}), or take a more analytical or academic tone. When a topic on the main site hints at something more, you’ll often see a link pointing here.
