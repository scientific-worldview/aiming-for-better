### Protection For Beliefs

There is a glaring **double standard** at the heart of our society’s [moral]({{ "/case-studies/morality/" | relative_url }}) and [legal]({{ "/case-studies/law/theory/" | relative_url }}) framework. If you hold a [belief]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#beliefs) based on ancient texts, untestable supernatural claims, or [subjective]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }}) [dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dogma), [society]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/" | relative_url }}) demands that your belief be treated with utmost _reverence_{: .bold}. Yet, a [worldview]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#scientific-worldview) *carefully arrived at* through meticulous _thought_{: .bold}, critical _inquiry_{: .bold}, [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}), and the [scientific method]({{ "/articles/science/ebsm/" | relative_url }}) receives no such cultural [deference]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#deference).

![Beliefs]({{ "/assets/images/posts/beliefs.webp" | relative_url }})

Technically, legal instruments like the UN Charter of [Rights]({{ "/case-studies/politics/governance/rights/" | relative_url }}), the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR), and the UK’s Equality Act 2010 group "philosophical belief" alongside [religion]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#religion). It is true that antiquated UK blasphemy laws were finally repealed in the 21st century. But in practice, the scales remain heavily tipped. Hate speech laws, institutional policies, and societal [norms]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/norms/" | relative_url }}) constantly shield unevidenced, untestable beliefs from rigorous challenge, treating them as [sacred]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#sacred) cows. Meanwhile, the scientific worldview is frequently dismissed, ignored, or treated as an inconvenience.

This deference to unevidenced belief is not harmless; it is the foundation of [_coercive control_]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#coercion). I know this intimately. Growing up in a _Pentecostal_{: .neon} household, I was explicitly [told]({{ "/guide/obstacles/being-told/" | relative_url }}) what to believe about the nature of reality. Even as a young [teenager]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/adolescence/" | relative_url }}), after I had critically examined and rejected these [narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}), I was forced into compliance. I had to pretend to believe, coerced by the very real prospect of physical or mental [mistreatment]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#mistreatment). Hence, I regard familial [brainwashing]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#brainwashing) with untestable belief (whether religious or secular; and whether the member believes or not) to be [neglect and abuse]({{ "/case-studies/society/normal-living/neglect-and-abuse/" | relative_url }}).

But this brand of coercive control does not end in childhood, nor is it confined to theocratic states or authoritarian regimes like Afghanistan, Iran or North Korea. It thrives right here in the Western World, often disguised as institutional benevolence.

### Protecting Indoctrination

One of the most pervasive forms of this disguised benevolence is found in how we raise and educate our youth. This project frequently laments the demise of the ancestral clan—a _horizontal_{: .cool}, _organic structure_{: .bold} that once provided children with a rich, diverse network of [mentorship]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#mentorship), age-disparate (rather than [age-segregated]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#age-segregation)) peering, and empirical learning. In its absence, modern society relies on institutional "patches" to *simulate community*: schools, youth organizations like Scouting, and churches. 

To be clear, the community aspect of these patches can sometimes be highly beneficial. When a movement like Scouting teaches a developing human practical, objective skills—how to tie a knot, how to survive outdoors, or how to cooperate as a team—it provides immense value. These are empirical, reality-based skill sets that build genuine competence, and this should be celebrated.

But all too often, these institutions serve as protected vehicles for rigid [indoctrination]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#brainwashing). Because they lack the diverse, organic mentorship of the ancient clan, they frequently impose just **one story**. Instead of being taught how to navigate the world through inquiry, children are taught a singular, untestable [narrative]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}). They are fed subjective [ideologies]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#ideology) and expected to revere unevidenced [dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dogma) as absolute truth. 

The defenders of these institutions often attempt **a false equivalence**, claiming that teaching a child [critical thinking]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/" | relative_url }}) or [the scientific method]({{ "/guide/first-principles/scientific-method/" | relative_url }}) is just "another form of indoctrination". This is a profound, manipulative _falsehood_{: .warn}. The scientific method is *not a belief system*; it is _a tool_{: .bold}. Teaching a child how to demand [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}), how to test reality, and how to logically deconstruct a claim _is an objective skill set—exactly like teaching them how to pitch a tent or build a fire_{: .mark}. 

Pushing an unevidenced belief system onto a developing mind, while actively withholding the tools of critical inquiry, should never be culturally celebrated or protected as **"moral guidance"**. From a [scientific worldview]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#scientific-worldview), it must be recognised for what it actually is: intellectual [neglect and psychological abuse]({{ "/case-studies/society/normal-living/neglect-and-abuse/" | relative_url }}). We must stop _protecting_{: .bold} this _indoctrination_{: .warn} under the *cowardly banner* of **"respecting beliefs"** and actively combat it.

Also, consider the [justice system]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#justice-and-injustice) in the United Kingdom. As explored in our [Pseudoscience]({{ "/case-studies/pseudoscience/" | relative_url }}) series, in particular the page regarding [Offending Behaviour Programs]({{ "/case-studies/pseudoscience/offender-treatment-industry/" | relative_url }}), many prisoners are subjected to a modern form of **coercive control**. History is a _blood-soaked_{: .warn} *testament* to what happens when people in [power]({{ "/case-studies/politics/governance/power/" | relative_url }}) enforce beliefs without the requirement of [objective]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }}) evidence. We do not have to look far to see the legacy of this mindset:

- _Trials by Ordeal:_{: .bullet} Where guilt or innocence was determined by surviving boiling water or grasping hot iron, trusting "God" to intervene.

- _The Burning of Witches:_{: .bullet} Thousands of innocent women and men tortured and executed based on mass hysteria and the untestable claims of the Malleus Maleficarum.

- _The Inquisition:_{: .bullet} Where heretics were broken on the rack for daring to question church dogma.

- _Lysenkoism in the USSR:_{: .bullet} Where the evidence-based science of genetics was outlawed in favour of Marxist ideology, resulting in agricultural collapse and the starvation of millions.

- _Medical Atrocities:_{: .bullet} From drilling holes in skulls (trepanation) to release "demons", to lobotomies performed on subjective behavioral assessments.

Therefore, this project takes a definitive, uncompromising stance: If you dictate to someone an untestable belief that has no evidence, and use power or coercion to enforce it, you are committing an _evil act_{: .warn}. **Period**. It is entirely irrelevant whether you believe you are "trying to do your best" or have "good intentions".
