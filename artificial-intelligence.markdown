### The Role of Artificial Intelligence

_Transparency_{: .cool} is a core principle of this project. If we are to advocate for an [evidence-based]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}), [rational]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#rational) approach to the world, we must be entirely _honest_{: .bold} about *how this website was created*. To that end, I want to be upfront: the text and structure you see across these pages were *brought to life* with the _assistance_{: .bold} of **artificial intelligence**.

### The Origin of the Ideas

While AI was *utilized as a tool* in this _project_{: .bold}, the foundational ideas and the overarching philosophy are **entirely my own**. They have been *germinating for a long time*, stretching back to my [teenage years]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/adolescence/" | relative_url }}).

Growing up in a [religious]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#religion) household, I was handed a specific set of [narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }}) to explain reality. As I grew older, I began to _question_{: .bold}, and eventually _reject_{: .warn}, those *inherited stories*. But that initial _skepticism_{: .bold} didn't stop at religion; it naturally _extended_{: .bold} to questioning the broader stories that [society]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/" | relative_url }}) tells itself. I started reading deeply—diving into philosophy, science, and history—_and began forming the conceptual framework that underpins this site._{: .mark} The core themes you will find here are the result of that **lifelong personal journey**:

- The _necessity_{: .bold} of a [clean slate]({{ "/guide/clean-slate/" | relative_url }}) and arguing from [first principles]({{ "/guide/first-principles/" | relative_url }})

- The _importance_{: .bold} of learning from our [human evolutionary history]({{ "/guide/human-history/" | relative_url }})

- _Recognizing_{: .bold} the power of [invented societal narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }})

- _Identifying_{: .bold} [evolutionary mismatches]({{ "/case-studies/mismatches/" | relative_url }}) in *modern life*

- _Confronting_{: .bold} cultural and legal [lock-ins]({{ "/case-studies/locked-in/" | relative_url }})

### The Writing and Editorial Process

I want to be *very clear* about what **"AI assistance"** actually looked like in the creation of this project. It was not a passive process of generating text; it was a _rigorous_{: .bold}, _combative_{: .warn}, and *highly iterative* _collaboration_{: .bold} in which I served as the *author*, *director*, and *final judge*.

Across the hundreds of pages on this site, _I have personally written many thousands of words._{: .mark} The process varied heavily from page to page:

- _Direct Drafting:_{: .bullet} Many pages began as _complete_{: .bold} or _near-complete_{: .bold} drafts *written entirely by me*, which were then simply fed to the AI for *structural improvement* or *grammatical polish*.

- _Rigorous Iteration:_{: .bullet} When starting from a *blank page*, the _creation_{: .bold} process involved feeding the AI _numerous_{: .bold}, highly detailed _prompts_{: .bold} of *significant length*.

- _Active Dissent:_{: .bullet} _I rarely accepted an AI response on the first try._{: .mark} I frequently _disagreed_{: .warn} with the output, _insisted_{: .bold} upon *strict alterations*, and forced the machine to refine its logic until it perfectly matched my *intended arguments*.

- _Obsessive Refinement:_{: .bullet} Once a draft was "finished", the work was still not done. I have _returned_{: .bold} to almost every page on this site multiple times to manually strip away overly academic, pseudoscientific, or overly technical jargon. _My goal is for this project to be as accessible and web-friendly as possible_{: .mark}, requiring a *massive editorial effort* on my part. (In terms of *"massive editorial effort"*, you will notice the various _highlighting_{: .bold} of orange bold, green italic, yellow highlight, and so forth. I created these several highlights in CSS and it took me _several_{: .warn} weeks to apply, *deciding* which words/phrases should be "styled". I believe it makes the *website look better* but others might **think it OTT!**)

Note: If you want to remove highlighting if you think it is excessive, please do so in [Settings]({{ "/settings/" | relative_url }})

Even with the _immense power_{: .bold} of **artificial intelligence** acting as a sounding board and editor, _bringing this website to life still took well over a year of dedicated, solitary work_{: .mark}, and I intend for it to be an *ongoing, evolving project*.

### The Message is What Matters

Ultimately, I make **no apologies** for using *every tool at my disposal* to build this platform. Without AI, the **sheer volume** of this work might have taken me *several more years to complete*, if I had ever managed to finish it at all.

But the **method** of this website's creation is *secondary to its purpose*. _The most important thing about this project is the message itself._{: .mark} I want to impart the *urgent need* for [critical thinking]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/" | relative_url }}). I want to _advocate_{: .bold} for the *widespread application* of [the evidence-based scientific method]({{ "/guide/first-principles/scientific-method/" | relative_url }}). I am certain that these are not just *academic concepts*, but the only **reliable tools** we have to *improve the human condition* at a faster pace than history has previously allowed. If AI can _help_{: .bold} deliver that message to a wider audience, _then it has served its purpose perfectly._{: .mark}

If you want to _discover this message_{: .bold}, *please enjoy this website*. I advise starting with the [Thinking Guide]({{ "/guide/" | relative_url }}) and then move onto [Case Studies]({{ "/case-studies/" | relative_url }}). _I really hope you enjoy this website and that it offers you plenty of food for thought._{: .mark}
