### Overview

When your life (or freedom, or future) depends on getting the answer right, **only** reliable, [empirical evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}) matters.

- *Not an* **"expert"**.

- *Not the* **majority**.

- *Not* [tradition]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#tradition).

- *Not* [beliefs]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#beliefs) or **emotions**.

Just [evidence]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#evidence)

### Why Other "Explanations" Are Unreliable

"Explanations" not _grounded in evidence_{: .bold}—whether from tradition, [authority]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#authority), or [consensus]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#consensus)—are shaped by:

- _Cultural Bias_{: .bullet}: Traditions reflect [values]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/values/" | relative_url }}) of specific groups, *not universal truths*.

- _Cognitive Bias:_{: .bullet} Human minds are _flawed_{: .warn}—we cherry-pick confirming data, overlook inconvenient [facts]({{ "/guide/first-principles/correct-answers/" | relative_url }}), and rely on gut feelings.

- _Power Dynamics:_{: .bullet} Authorities often speak from *positions of influence*, not **objectivity**. Their answers may serve the [status quo]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#status-quo).

- _Resistance to Change:_{: .bullet} Consensus and tradition tend to *entrench beliefs*, even when _contradicted_{: .warn} by new data.

---

### Evidence vs. Authority

Yes, you can consult a doctor or specialist—*but don’t stop there*. Ask for **the evidence** behind their advice:

What *supports* this diagnosis?

How *strong* is the evidence?

Are there *alternatives*?

Authority may offer order and discipline in an army. But for truth-seeking, *authority is not the final word*. **Evidence is**.

_Experts are fallible_{: .warn}. They may unknowingly be guided by *tradition*, *assumption*, or even a *desire to appear confident*. Your job isn’t to reject authority—it’s to **demand evidence** from it.

---

### Evidence vs. Consensus

Widespread agreement *doesn't guarantee truth*. _History proves this repeatedly._{: .mark}

For millenia, it seemed "obvious" that time was absolute. Then Einstein proposed that time is relative and the speed of light is constant—a radical idea, now *backed by mountains of evidence*. You can bet your life that time slows as you approach light speed.

Consensus crumbled. Evidence stood.

---

### Evidence vs. Tradition

Traditions often outlive their usefulness and _resist correction_{: .warn}.

When it became clear the Earth orbited the sun, it still took **centuries** for [dogma]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#dogma) to yield to data.

Many people cling to *comforting* [narratives]({{ "/case-studies/narratives/" | relative_url }})—heaven, hell, or karmic justice—because they’re *cherished* or *inherited*. _Letting them go is hard_{: .bold}. But when [survival]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#survival) or truth is at stake, **we must**.

---

### Why This Matters

Throughout history, many **wrong explanations** have led to great _suffering_{: .warn} and [oppression]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#oppression). And yet, *there are still harmful, wrong explanations*. Surely, we must root them out? And yet, people are often hurt, ridiculed, ostracised or oppressed *merely for questioning the evidence*. **Why?** [Testability]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }}) and keeping an [open mind]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#open-mind) demands that we **must** question even the best evidence. It's what leads to _reliability_{: .bold}.

The article [→ Wrong Explanations]({{ "/articles/history/wrong-explanations/" | relative_url }}) offers a number of examples of authoritative, widely-held (consensus), and/or traditional explanations succumbing to the evidence.

## When Evidence Is All That Matters

Imagine you must get the answer right. You’re facing real consequences—life, death, health, or [liberty]({{ "/case-studies/law/pragmatic/liberty/" | relative_url }}).

Suddenly:

> What your [parents]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#parental-ownership) taught you: *out the window.*

> What the priest or teacher or [therapist]({{ "/guide/human-history/therapy-culture/" | relative_url }}) said: *gone.*

> What’s popular or traditional or emotionally satisfying: *irrelevant.*

_Only the Evidence Remains_{: .mark}
