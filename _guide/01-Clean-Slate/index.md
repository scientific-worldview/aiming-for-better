### Overview

What if your life _depended_{: .bold} on getting the answer right?

Not just any answer — but the answer to a serious question about truth, people, policy, good and bad, or how the world works. Imagine your safety, your family, your [freedom]({{ "/case-studies/law/pragmatic/liberty/" | relative_url }}), or your future all *hanging in the balance*.

Would you still rely on your [usual beliefs]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#beliefs)?

Would you still trust your gut, your upbringing, or the way things have “always been”? Would you allow your thinking to be done for you by profit and sensationalist motivated tabloid media or power/status-hungry politicians?

*Most people would say no*. If the stakes were high enough, they’d be willing to let go of _cherished beliefs_{: .neon} — even deeply held convictions — to get to the [correct answer]({{ "/guide/first-principles/correct-answers/" | relative_url }}). They’d want evidence, [testing]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }}), and objectivity.

![I Don't Know]({{ "/assets/images/guide/first-principles/idontknow.webp" | relative_url }})

That’s the [clean slate]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#clean-slate) challenge at the heart of this website.

### Why Is This So Hard?

We all grow up immersed in a [worldview]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#worldview) — a mix of [culture]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#culture), [religion]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#religion), [values]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/values/" | relative_url }}), habits, and unspoken assumptions. You can’t help it. It’s shaped by *where you were born*, what you were _taught_{: .warn}, and the *time in history you happen to live in*.

Even if you consider yourself a [critical thinker]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/" | relative_url }}) or a [rational]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#rational) skeptic, your beliefs are still likely _influenced_{: .warn} by all kinds of personal, social, and emotional forces.

And the hard truth?

Most of us _start with conclusions we like_{: .bold} — and then *go looking for reasons to justify them*.

- _confirmation bias_{: .bullet} We tend to seek information that *confirms what we already believe* while ignoring contradictory evidence.

- _authority bias_{: .bullet} We often accept information simply because it comes from someone in a position of [authority]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#authority).

- _emotional reasoning_{: .bullet} We *mistake strong feelings for evidence* of truth.

### What If You Had to Start Over?

Imagine being forced to strip away every bias and external influence. Imagine having to rebuild your worldview from scratch — not based on what’s popular, [traditional]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#tradition), or comfortable, but on what can be **justified** clearly, logically, and with [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }}).

That’s what this project invites you to try:

- Identify our existing [biases]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/cognitive-biases/" | relative_url }})
- Question our *assumptions*
- Create space for **new perspectives**
- Develop [intellectual humility]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/intellectual-humility/" | relative_url }})

Not because it’s easy.

_Because it matters._{: .subtle}

### What Would You Have to Set Aside?

If your life (or something equally important) depended on it, you'd have to set aside things like:

- _Religious beliefs and dogma_{: .bullet} – [sacred]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#sacred) texts, divine authority, supernatural claims
- _Cultural conditioning_{: .bullet} – *upbringing*, customs, community *values*
- _Political bias_{: .bullet} – party loyalty, [ideological]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#ideology) spin, winning votes, *staying in [power]({{ "/case-studies/politics/governance/power/" | relative_url }})
- _Nationalism or patriotism_{: .bullet} – prioritizing one's country over [objective]({{ "/guide/first-principles/intersubjective/" | relative_url }}) [facts]({{ "/guide/first-principles/correct-answers/" | relative_url }})
- _Legal frameworks_{: .bullet} – outdated, unjust or politically correct [laws]({{ "/case-studies/law/theory/" | relative_url }})
- _Career interests_{: .bullet} – career stability, *job security*, industry [norms]({{ "/case-studies/society/sociology/norms/" | relative_url }})
- _Social status_{: .bullet} – reputation, recognition, *fear of losing face*
- _Money_{: .bullet} – salary, wealth, business profits, industry [lobbying]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#lobbying), financial incentives
- _Emotion_{: .bullet} – nostalgia, [fear]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#fear), anger, pride, hope, sentimentality
- _Groupthink_{: .bullet} – loyalty to peers, tribe, or cause
- _Tradition_{: .bullet} – “this is how we’ve always done it”
- _Conformity_{: .bullet} – fear of standing out or *facing rebuke*
- _Peer pressure_{: .bullet} – aligning with friends, colleagues, or social groups
- _Media noise_{: .bullet} – headlines, [misinformation]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#misinformation), [propaganda]({{ "/guide/human-history/propaganda/" | relative_url }}), social media influence
- _Institutional inertia_{: .bullet} – “that’s just how we do things here”
- _Psychological comfort_{: .bullet} – avoiding [cognitive dissonance]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#cognitive-dissonance) or distressing truths
- _Ego and pride_{: .bullet} – *unwillingness to admit being wrong*
- _Reputation and public image_{: .bullet} – fear of embarrassment or losing credibility
- _Motives, Missions and Agendas_{: .bullet} – “saving souls”, “protecting the public”, “victims voices”
- _Family expectations_{: .bullet} – *pressures from* [parents]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#parental-ownership), spouse, or community
- _Pop culture_{: .bullet} – celebrity opinions, social trends
- _Superstition and pseudoscience_{: .bullet} – astrology, alternative medicine, conspiracy theory
- _Moral or ethical assumptions_{: .bullet} – personal values overriding evidence
- _Wishful thinking_{: .bullet} – the way we want the world to be...and *the way it ought to be made to be!*

## So What Comes Next?

If you could set all that aside — *truly wipe the slate clean* — what kind of thinking would remain?

What kind of framework could you trust?

This site proposes a way forward: *an approach to evaluation and decision-making* that starts not from tradition or ideology, but from observation, testability, and reasoning — _an evidence-based mindset_{: .mark} for making sense of the world.

> *If you had to get it right — what would your worldview look like?*
