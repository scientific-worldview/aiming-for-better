### Overview

We all see the world *through a lens* — a set of ideas, [beliefs]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#beliefs), and assumptions we rarely stop to question.

This is what philosophers sometimes call a [worldview]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#worldview): the mental ["software"]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#operating-system-analogy) through which we interpret reality, make judgments, and decide what matters.

But here’s the catch:

**You didn’t choose your worldview**

*Not really*.

You _inherited_{: .warn} it — from your [parents]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#parental-ownership), your [culture]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#culture), your school and [education]({{ "/case-studies/society/social-structures/education/" | relative_url }}), your [religion]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#religion) (or rejection of it), your time in history, even the media you consume.

![Herd]({{ "/assets/images/guide/follow-me.webp" | relative_url }})

If you’d been born in Ancient Sparta or 19th-century Japan, you’d almost certainly believe very different things today.

---

### Why This Matters

Worldviews shape how we [vote]({{ "/case-studies/politics/democracy/boundary-of-the-ballot/" | relative_url }}), how we treat others, what we believe is true — and even what we consider “common sense”.

And while science has expanded our understanding of the universe, most of us still walk around carrying worldviews shaped more by [tradition]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#tradition), tribe, and emotion than by _clear thinking_{: .cool} or evidence. 

**It can't be helped.**

We all grow up in [The Herd]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#the-herd). Thinking along with the herd gives us comfort, [certainty]({{ "/case-studies/politics/dysfunction/certainty/" | relative_url }}), and *a sense of belonging*.

That’s not an insult — it’s a human reality called [Groupthink]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#groupthink). But it’s one worth challenging.

![Feynman]({{ "/assets/images/guide/feynman.webp" | relative_url }})

### What If You Could Start From Scratch?

Imagine *setting aside* the assumptions of your upbringing.

Imagine [wiping the slate clean]({{ "/guide/clean-slate/" | relative_url }}).

What would your worldview look like if you **rebuilt it carefully** — using observation, using [testable claims]({{ "/guide/first-principles/testability/" | relative_url }}), using [logical thinking]({{ "/guide/thinking-for-yourself/" | relative_url }}) and based on reliable [evidence]({{ "/guide/first-principles/evidence/" | relative_url }})?

This website is a [thought experiment]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}#experiment) in exactly that.

---

### Who Is This Site For?

This project is for anyone who’s ever asked:

- “Why do people believe such different things?”

- “What can I really trust in the information I get?”

- “Is it possible to make **better decisions** — individually or as a society?”

You don’t need a science degree. You just need a willingness to think clearly, stay curious, and be open to challenging what you take for granted — including the assumptions behind this very website.

## Bottom Line

You already have a worldview.

The question is: Is it helping you think clearly and live wisely — or just keeping you comfortable within _The Herd?_{: .retro}

This website is here to explore what changes *when we ask that question seriously*.
