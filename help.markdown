### Suggestions

1. If you have trouble reading this website and you don't wear glasses, why not use the zoom function found in the "Settings" of all web browsers? For example, you could change the zoom level from 100% to 120% or more.

### Big Words

In general, I try to avoid big words, but sometimes a certain word is the most appropriate to use. If you encounter something you don't recognise, hopefully there is a definiton in the [Glossary]({{ "/glossary/" | relative_url }}).
